## Short Note On How I Got There And Tools Used

```text
I treated the assignment as a recurring FDE problem, not a one-time product audit. I started from the employee commute journey in the case prompt, then reviewed public signals from the MoveInSync Google Play listing, AppBrain, Chrome-Stats review samples, Apple App Store, competitor listings, and Google Play documentation. I grouped negative review themes by commute stage and severity, then prioritized fixes that reduce high-stress rating events: booking confidence, live tracking freshness, no-show recovery, OTP visibility, app reliability, and support closure.

I used AI tools to speed up research structuring, theme clustering, rating-math reasoning, and document formatting. The final recommendation is packaged as a field memo, a short PPT, and an editable Markdown version. My proposed long-term solution is a reusable Ratings Room system that ingests Play reviews, classifies them by trip stage/theme/severity/owner, creates tickets for severe issues, and gives Product, Engineering, Ops, and Customer Success a weekly operating view.
```
